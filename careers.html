How to read the scores

Likelihood is a rough planning estimate of the chance of landing one role of that type within about two years of applying seriously. It assumes the PhD is finished with 2–3 publications and combines fit with the research, size of the applicant pool, language and the right to work. Difficulty (1–4) is how much effort and preparation a strong application needs. These are judgement calls, not statistics. Revise them as the profile grows.

A year-by-year plan that keeps every door open

Year 4

Oct 2028 → viva

  • Submit Paper 4 (Indonesia). Prepare a book proposal if the supervisors agree.

  • Prepare three CV versions: academic (publications first), policy (impact and briefs first) and industry (methods and case studies first).

After the PhD
first 2–3 years

  • Keep publishing and keep one foot in each sector, e.g. an academic post plus advisory work, or a policy role plus an honorary research affiliation.
  • Track the likelihood scores: they rise quickly with each publication, grant and policy citation.
05 — RIGHT TO WORK, BRIEFLY

What each country requires

Correct as far as public sources show in October 2026. Always check official sources before acting.

UK

Graduate · Skilled Worker · Global Talent

PhD graduates get 3 years on the Graduate route (the cut to 18 months from 1 Jan 2027 applies to bachelor’s and master’s graduates). Skilled Worker needs a sponsor. Global Talent (British Academy endorsement) needs no sponsor.

Indonesia

Home market

No visa barrier. Civil-service roles (BRIN, ministries) are recruited through the national CPNS/PPPK cycle; universities and companies hire directly.

Europe

Researcher permit · EU Blue Card

Universities usually sponsor a researcher permit. Industry uses the EU Blue Card. Permanent EU-institution posts require EU citizenship. Local language often matters for policy roles.

US

J-1 · H-1B · O-1

Postdocs often come on J-1. Universities are exempt from the H-1B cap, but industry roles go through the H-1B lottery. O-1 needs a strong record of distinction.

Sources: UKCISA: Graduate route · Freeths: changes to graduate visas (2026)

Career notes are general guidance, not legal or immigration advice. Openings change daily; check each posting’s deadline.
