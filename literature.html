Literature, rated for your research

Papers and books that speak to your three questions, your theory and your method, from 486 candidates found in OpenAlex. Each is rated for relevance (read from its abstract), influence and recency. Paywalled ones open through your Goldsmiths login.

How the ratings work

Overall = 60% relevance + 20% influence + 20% recency, each out of 10. Relevance was judged by reading each abstract against RQ1–RQ3, your theory and your method; anything under 4 was left out. Influence is citations per year since publication, on a log scale. Recency favours 2024–2026 work, except for the classics your framework rests on. “Likely in your draft” matches surname and year against your upgrade text, so check it. Paywalled papers: open the DOI link and choose “Access through your institution” → Goldsmiths, or search the title in the Goldsmiths library. Data from OpenAlex.
