The Wellbeing Gazette

Goldsmiths · MCCSPhD upgrade · Oct 2026Work in progress

Who is made responsible when we hand our wellbeing to a machine?

News discourse on AI chatbots, 2023–2026, and the Indonesian case

The scale

1M+

ChatGPT users a week who show explicit indicators of possible suicidal planning (OpenAI, Oct 2025: ~0.15% of ~800M).

The gap

Weeks-long NHS waits in the UK. In Indonesia: stigma, private psychiatric insurance, and pasung.

The argument

AI companions copy the form of the pure relationship but remove its reciprocity. Users look for wellbeing in the same relationship that keeps producing the need for it. News decides who must manage that loop, and it does so before regulation arrives.

Three strands

RQ1 The Guardian, 86 articles identified so far.

RQ2 WSJ “Family & Tech”, 20 columns.

RQ3 Riliv founders and psychologists, and HIMPSI, in Indonesia.

Method

van Dijk news discourse analysis · reflexive thematic analysis

03 — THE PROCESS

From a 15,000-word chapter to a poster read in 90 seconds

Choose one claim

Write the sentence you want someone to remember from across the room, e.g. “News places responsibility for chatbot wellbeing in the home before regulation arrives.”

Choose one figure

Pick from the figure options. Use the arc for RQ1, the dot timeline for RQ2, the four norms for RQ3 and the loop for theory.

Cut to 300 words

Three boxes of about 60 words (question, data, what we look for) plus the title. Anything longer belongs in the paper.

Lay it out in a Z

Title top left, figure in the middle, takeaway bottom right. One palette: teal, gold, terracotta.

Test at 25%

Print an A4 proof (the buttons above) and read it at arm’s length. If the title and figure don’t carry the claim, simplify.

Prepare the 60-second pitch

Problem (1M+ a week) → question (who is made responsible?) → design (three strands) → why Indonesia. End with an invitation to talk.

Pick the venue

UK: MeCCSA and the Goldsmiths MCCS research events. International: ICA, IAMCR, ECREA, AoIR. Asia: regional communication association meetings.

Reuse it

The same figure and claim become the paper abstract, a conference slide, a LinkedIn or Bluesky post and a line on the CV.
Posters show design and data only. No findings are claimed before analysis.
